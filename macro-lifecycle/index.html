Key Due Diligence Insights from the Macro Planning Lifecycle
1. The Post-Consent "Delivery Chasm" (147.0 GW Stalled): Over 71% of all consented capacity (147.0 GW across 5,948 projects) currently sits in Awaiting Construction status. The primary bottleneck to UK net zero is no longer local planning committees, but transmission and distribution grid connection queues (NESO TEC Register).
2. Technology Dominance in the Queue: Battery Storage (BESS) represents 147.8 GW of total pipeline, with 62.3 GW already consented and stalled awaiting transmission connection dates. Solar PV represents 68.2 GW, of which 38.4 GW is awaiting build.
3. Geographical Polarization: Scotland hosts 74.1% (45.4 GW) of all Onshore Wind pipeline, while England dominates Solar PV (60.3 GW) and BESS (102.0 GW), reflecting differing land availability, wind resource, and planning frameworks.
