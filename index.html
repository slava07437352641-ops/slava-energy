Key Due Diligence Insights
1. The 147 GW Delivery Chasm: Over 71% of all consented clean energy capacity (147.0 GW across 5,948 projects) sits in Awaiting Construction status. The primary gatekeeper to Net Zero buildout is transmission connection queues (NESO TEC Register).
2. BESS & Solar Surge: Battery Storage (BESS) represents 147.8 GW of total pipeline, with 62.3 GW consented. Solar PV totals 68.2 GW, with 38.4 GW consented awaiting construction.
3. Regional Specialisation: Scotland commands 74.1% (45.4 GW) of all Onshore Wind pipeline, while England commands the majority of Solar PV and BESS capacity.
